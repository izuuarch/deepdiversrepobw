Our Privacy Policy - BW Tech

This policy explains how BW Tech collects, uses, protects, and handles your Personally Identifiable Information (PII) as required by global privacy regulations.

Introduction

BW Tech (referred to as "BW Tech," "we," "us," or "our") operates the website bwtechglobal.com and provides expert marine and offshore solutions. This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Data when you use our website and interact with our services.

Information We Collect

We collect several different types of information for various purposes to provide and improve our services to you.

Personal Data (Directly Provided)

  • Contact Information: Name, Email Address, specifically when you fill out a Contact Form or request a Quote.
  • Inquiry Details: Information you provide regarding your project, service needs (e.g., vessel name, port of operation, required inspection class), and budget.

Usage Data (Automatically Collected)

  • Log Data: Information your browser sends when you visit our website, such as your computer's Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser type, browser version, the pages you visit, the time and date of your visit, and the time spent on those pages.
  • Tracking Data: Data collected via Cookies, Google Analytics, and other third-party services to analyze user behavior for continuous website improvement.

How We Use Your Data

We use the collected data for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain our services, including responding to your requests for quotes and information.
  • To contact you with service updates, marketing materials, and newsletters, where you have consented.
  • To process and manage bids for maritime and offshore projects.
  • To monitor the usage of our website and analyze trends to improve user experience.
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues and ensure the security of our website.

Legal Requirements

BW Tech may disclose your Personal Data in the good faith belief that such action is necessary to:

  • Comply with a legal obligation or court order.
  • Protect and defend the rights or property of BW Tech.
  • Prevent or investigate possible wrongdoing in connection with the service.

Your Data Protection Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your Personal Data:

Right to Access

The right to request copies of your Personal Data.

Right to Rectification

The right to request that we correct any information you believe is inaccurate or incomplete.

Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")

The right to request that we erase your Personal Data, under certain conditions.
