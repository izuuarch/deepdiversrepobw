About Us

Delivering Excellence in Marine and Industrial Services

At BW Tech Global Logistics Services, we provide top-tier inspection, repair, and maintenance to ensure safety and efficiency in the marine, oil/gas, and underwater industries. With our certified expertise and client trust, we uphold the highest standards to safeguard operations and promote sustainability.

We offer a range of specialized services in the marine, oil/gas, and underwater industries, including:

  • Underwater inspection and surveys of ships and offshore units
  • Hull cleaning, propeller polishing, and underwater maintenance
  • Pipeline and wellhead inspection and maintenance
  • SBM inspection, wreck removal, and underwater welding
  • Inspection and maintenance of storage tanks, life rafts, and fire extinguishers
